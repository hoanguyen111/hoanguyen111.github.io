Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "Quản Lý Chi Phí Xây Nhà" ("the App") collects, uses, and protects user information when using the application.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

The App is designed to help users manage construction expenses. Most data entered into the App is stored locally on your device.

We may collect the following types of information:

User Provided Information

  • Construction project names
  • Expense records
  • Notes and descriptions
  • Dates and cost amounts

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Automatically Collected Information

The App may use third-party services that automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Anonymous usage data
  • Crash reports

These services help improve app stability and performance.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect anonymous information including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App interaction data

This information is used only to provide relevant advertisements.

For more information, please review Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage

All project and expense data created by users is stored locally on the user's device.

The developer does not have access to this information.

Users are responsible for backing up their own data.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Security

We value your trust in providing information and strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect it.

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
